# Your own logo and soundtrack

Drop files here before building and they become the defaults:

- `logo.png` — shown in the title sequence, the corner of every film, and the home screen. Transparent background works best.
- `music.m4a` (or `.mp3`, `.wav`, `.aiff`) — the film's soundtrack. Must be a file you own.

You can also pick both from **Settings → Your logo and soundtrack** on the phone, which overrides these.
